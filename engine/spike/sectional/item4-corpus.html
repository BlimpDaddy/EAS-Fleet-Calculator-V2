Does the drag map fit the historical record?

Every body in the repository with real aerodynamic evidence, rescored through one frozen pipeline under one declared convention. The Göttingen ellipsoids were revealed against predictions committed first. Nothing was recalibrated while looking.

The map is not merely sparse — it is being contradicted in both directions at once. Streamlined bodies sit on it; prolate fat bodies sit 4.7–9.9× below it; the oblate ellipsoid sits 24% above it. One proxy→pressure law is pricing at least three aerodynamic families.

The six frozen questions

Q1 — one smooth relationship? No.

Same-proxy bodies differ in residual by an order of magnitude. TN-614 non-knots land within 15% of the line; C-class and the virgin prolate land 4.7–9.9× under it.

Q2 — distinct families? Yes, opposite signs.

Prolate fat bodies far below the line, the oblate above it, streamlined on it. No single curve can satisfy points that disagree about its sign.

Q3 — do ribbons explain the scatter? Partially.

Oblate/prolate residuals differ 14.5× where the scalar separates 1.5×; F2a spreads 2.1× — better, still short. A4 fires on exactly the two square bodies in the corpus. Family identity explains the split best.

Q4 — do warnings track error? On pressure, yes.

Every 4.7–9.9× miss is a REDUCED-confidence row; SUPPORTED rows sit within ~±20%.

Q5 — is the mid-map gap real? Worse than sparse.

The 0.18–0.82 span had zero measured anchors. Ten-plus corpus bodies now populate it — and split by family rather than scattering around the line.

Q6 — new anchors available? None cleanly.

The Göttingen pair is drag-crisis-adjacent Re; TR-291 is permanently barred; TR-397 is appendaged and traced. The high-Re mid-map desert remains, and saying so is the deliverable.

Estimator-response evidence, not aerodynamic validation. The interpretation ruling — smoother global curve, family-specific mappings, or analogue interpolation — is taken separately from this page.
