  THE MECHANISM: a flattened section is resolved by fewer cells across its
  thin axis, and the deficit GROWS through the aft closure as the section
  shrinks. That is precisely the region the aft-penalty descriptor reads.
  Through the mid-aft closure the ellipse runs 0.8-1.9% low against the circles
  <=0.11%; the worst errors, near the tips where sections are tiny, are 5.28%
  for the circle and 15.57% for the ellipse. softAft is a THRESHOLD functional
  of the equivalent-radius profile, so an error concentrated there does not
  average out -- it emerges as the 15.5% spread in compositeV1 that P1 predicted
  could not happen.
